# v0.7 Range Story Engine Notes

Branch: `nci-story-v0.7-range-engine`

## Purpose

This patch starts the Range Story Engine.

Range is not noise. Range is a complete market chapter that must stay connected to the higher-timeframe story.

## What v0.7 adds

- Preliminary range high / range low detection.
- Range midline.
- Range width classification.
- Range position classification:
  - near range high,
  - near range low,
  - range middle / noise.
- Range story states:
  - range rotation up,
  - range rotation down,
  - range high decision,
  - range low decision,
  - fakeout above range,
  - fakeout below range,
  - valid breakout candidate,
  - valid breakdown candidate.
- Range panel text.
- Range lines on chart.

## Mandatory NCI range law

```text
Range can begin after valid breakout, valid pullback, failed continuation, liquidity sweep, or compression after impulse.
```

Range edges are decision zones.

Range middle is low-conviction/noise.

## HTF hierarchy rule preserved

Range must be interpreted through the parent timeframe story.

Example:

```text
4H bearish continuation → price reaches LL → 1H range forms near LL
```

Correct story:

```text
1H range near 4H LL = pullback/accumulation/explanation area first.
Do not call bullish reversal until valid 4H invalidation or valid 4H reversal.
```

## Important limitation

This is still preliminary. Future versions must connect range breakout/fakeout to NCI breakout/pullback standards and SMC liquidity sweeps.
