# v0.8 NCI Breakout / Pullback Validation Engine Notes

Branch: `nci-story-v0.8-breakout-pullback`

## Purpose

This patch starts the NCI validation layer for breakout and pullback logic.

Until v0.7, breakout/range states were only candidates. v0.8 begins separating candidate movements from validated NCI pressure.

## What v0.8 adds

- Preliminary two-candle Maru pressure validation.
- Preliminary big Maru + small candle validation.
- Breakout-line validation against chart range high / range low.
- Pullback pressure classification.
- Range breakout candidate validation text.
- Range breakdown candidate validation text.
- Story panel output for breakout / pullback validation.

## NCI rules represented

### Breakout validation

```text
Breakout line can be recent high/low, key level, POI, or range border.
Two Maru breakout: both candles close beyond breakout line and candle 2 continues beyond candle 1.
Big Maru + small candle: first candle is strong, second candle holds beyond 50% of first candle, and body closes beyond breakout line.
```

### Pullback validation

```text
Pullback pressure can be represented by two Maru candles in pullback direction,
or big Maru + small candle structure with continuation pressure.
```
