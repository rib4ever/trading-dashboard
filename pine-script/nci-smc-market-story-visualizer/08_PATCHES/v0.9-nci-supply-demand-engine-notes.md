# v0.9 NCI Supply / Demand Engine Notes

Branch: `nci-story-v0.9-sd-engine`

## Purpose

This patch starts the NCI Supply / Demand Engine.

Supply and Demand is not drawn randomly. Zones must support the active story and should be filtered by relevance.

## What v0.9 adds

- Preliminary chart timeframe supply and demand zones.
- Preliminary master timeframe supply and demand zones.
- Zone type classification:
  - demand candidate,
  - supply candidate,
  - strongest demand candidate,
  - strongest supply candidate,
  - obsolete / broken zone candidate.
- Story panel SD context.
- Optional SD boxes on the chart.
- Story-relevant SD filtering principle.

## NCI SD principles represented

```text
Strongest SD = obvious imbalance, strong distance away, Maru pressure, immediate reaction.
Broken SD becomes obsolete.
Older / unrelated SD loses value.
Normal mode should show only story-relevant SD zones.
Debug mode can show more candidates later.
```
